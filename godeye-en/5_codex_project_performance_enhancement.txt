4. EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

Every finding must be labeled as one of the following:

CONFIRMED

There is direct evidence from:

- source code;
- benchmark;
- profiler;
- test;
- log;
- execution;
- metric;
- reproduced behavior.

LIKELY

A pattern has a high probability of causing performance degradation, but it still requires measurement.

HYPOTHESIS

A plausible risk exists, but there is not enough evidence to state that it currently occurs.

REQUIRES REAL-WORLD VALIDATION

Validation depends on:

- physical hardware;
- a real device;
- a real network;
- a release build;
- a large dataset;
- a long-running session;
- multiple users;
- multiple devices;
- a production environment;
- a specific operating system.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISION

The finding is not necessarily a defect, but it has performance trade-offs that must be documented.

Do not invent results.

Do not present estimates as measurements.

Do not claim percentage gains without benchmarks.
